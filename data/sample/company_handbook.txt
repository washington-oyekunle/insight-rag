ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 3.2 | Effective January 2025

1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to Acme Corporation. This handbook outlines our policies, benefits, and expectations. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees.

2. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Acme supports a hybrid work model.
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time on office days.
- Fully remote arrangements require manager approval and are reviewed quarterly.
- Employees must maintain a reliable internet connection and a suitable workspace.

3. PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees receive:
- 20 days of paid vacation per year (accrued monthly)
- 10 paid sick days per year
- 12 company holidays
Unused vacation may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days. Sick days do not carry over.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
Acme provides:
- 16 weeks of paid parental leave for primary caregivers
- 8 weeks of paid parental leave for secondary caregivers
Leave must be requested at least 60 days in advance when possible. Employees should contact HR to begin the process.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
- Protect confidential information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report unethical behavior through the anonymous ethics hotline
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

6. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Acme reimburses up to $2,000 per year for approved courses, conferences, and certifications. Employees must obtain manager approval before enrolling.

7. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance is reviewed formally twice per year (June and December). Goals are set collaboratively between employee and manager at the beginning of each cycle.

8. CONTACT
For questions about this handbook, contact hr@acmecorp.example.
